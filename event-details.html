International Overdose Awareness Day 2026

Venue & Location

Centre of Excellence Auditorium, Institute of Mental Health, Erragadda, Hyderabad

About the Event

International Overdose Awareness Day is the world’s largest annual campaign to end overdose, remember without stigma those who have died, and acknowledge the grief of family and friends left behind. Join us at the Centre of Excellence Auditorium, Institute of Mental Health, Erragadda for meaningful discussions, harm-reduction knowledge, healthcare expert insights, and community solidarity.

Key Highlights
Harm Reduction & Awareness
Mental Health Specialists Panel
Support & Safe Space Dialogue
Community Solidarity & Action
